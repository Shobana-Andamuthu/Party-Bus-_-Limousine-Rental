Chauffeur Standard

Velvet VIP Service Protocols

The strict 4-point operational standards followed by every Velvet master chauffeur.

Protocol 1: Professional Dress & Absolute Discretion Code
Every chauffeur is strictly uniformed in formal dark suits, ties, and gloves. All drivers sign non-disclosure agreements (NDAs) ensuring complete privacy for high-profile executive and celebrity guests.
Protocol 2: Flight Monitoring & Zero-Delay Meet & Greet
Our dispatch desk automatically syncs with live commercial and private flight radar. If your flight is delayed 2 hours, your chauffeur adjusts arrival seamlessly with luggage assistance inside arrivals.
Protocol 3: Cabin Temperature & Refreshment Tailoring
Prior to pickup, the cabin climate control is set to your preferred temperature. Stocked bar coolers, sparkling water, champagne flutes, and device chargers are pre-configured to your itinerary.
Protocol 4: Late-Night Doorstep Security Escort
For evening drop-offs, bachelorettes, and prom groups, our chauffeurs remain parked with headlights illuminating your entrance until every guest is safely inside their residence.
