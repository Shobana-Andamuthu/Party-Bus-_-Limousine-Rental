FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers before you reach out to our support team.

How soon can I book a party bus or limousine?

Same-day slots are often available. Choose your date inside our booking dashboard or call our concierge desk and we will confirm your vehicle within minutes.

Do you provide champagne glassware, ice, and bar setups?

Yes! Every Velvet vehicle includes built-in ice bars stocked with complimentary bottled water, ice cubes, and crystal glassware ready for your celebratory drinks.

Can I reschedule or cancel my reservation?

Free date changes are permitted up to 7 days before your event date. Deposits are fully refundable up to 14 days prior to departure.

Is my service area covered for multi-home pickups?

Yes! We cover the entire metropolitan and surrounding suburban areas. Custom pickup stops at multiple guest locations can be specified directly inside your reservation.
