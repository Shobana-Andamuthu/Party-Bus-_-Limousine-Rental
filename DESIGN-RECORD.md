# DESIGN RECORD — TEMPLATE 4: VELVET LUXURY CELEBRATIONS

## BUSINESS IDENTITY & TARGET AUDIENCE
- **Business**: VELVET - Premier Party Bus & Limousine Rental Service.
- **Target Audience**: Prom couples, bachelorette parties, brides & grooms, birthday planners, corporate executives, and VIP nightlife guests seeking celebratory group transport.

## VISUAL DIRECTION
- **Theme**: Deep Burgundy Red & Rich Crimson Modern Luxury (`#7A1018`, `#A71924`, `#C62832`, `#23070A`, `#FAF7F7`).
- **Brand Personality**: Bold, elegant, image-rich, celebratory, modern, and highly readable.
- **Zero Gold Guarantee**: Completely eliminated all previous champagne-gold and black-and-gold styling in favor of rich deep burgundy red accents.

## COLOR SYSTEM
- **Primary**: `#7A1018` (Deep Burgundy Red)
- **Secondary**: `#A71924` (Rich Crimson)
- **Accent**: `#C62832` (Premium Red)
- **Dark Background**: `#23070A` (Deep Wine)
- **Light Background**: `#FAF7F7` (Soft Warm Off-White)
- **Surface**: `#FFFFFF` (Pure White)
- **Text Primary**: `#171717` (Charcoal Black)
- **Muted Text**: `#667085` (Cool Gray)

## TYPOGRAPHY
- **Font Family**: Google Fonts `Outfit` (Modern, clean, legible sans-serif for headings and body).

## HOMEPAGE CONCEPTS
- **Home 1 (`index.html`)**: Fleet Red-Carpet Hero concept with quick vehicle reservation bar, 4-card fleet showcase, vertical package cards with high-contrast gradient scrims, split private chauffeur highlight, instant hourly rate estimator, stat counters, verified client reviews, and red carpet CTA banner. Exactly 8 sections.
- **Home 2 (`home-2.html`)**: Fullscreen Lounge & Nightlife Edition concept with ambient video/photo hero, 5-tile asymmetric bento grid layout gallery, horizontal onboard amenity cards, 4-step online booking timeline, fleet specification comparison table, FAQ accordion, testimonial quote slider, and bottom booking CTA banner. Exactly 8 sections.

## INNER PAGES (EXACTLY 7 SECTIONS EACH)
1. **Fleet (`fleet.html`)**: Filterable fleet catalog (limo, party-bus, sprinter, suv), passenger capacity guide, technical specs, safety certification, customization options, guest reviews, CTA.
2. **Packages (`packages.html`)**: Occasion collection cards (Bachelorette, Prom, Wedding, Corporate, Birthday), included perks, customization concierge, prom safety guarantee, package pricing tiers, testimonials, CTA.
3. **Services (`services.html`)**: Private chauffeur catalog (Hourly charter, Airport express, Event shuttles, City tours), service standards, airport terminal transfer feature, stats counter, booking guide, reviews, CTA.
4. **Pricing (`pricing.html`)**: Hourly rate cards, interactive live trip cost simulator, distance/zone flat rate table, no-hidden-fee guarantee, deposit/cancellation terms, client reviews, CTA.
5. **About (`about.html` - Exact Label "About")**: Brand story split, core values, in-house maintenance facility, milestones, leadership & concierge team, reputation reviews, CTA.
6. **Contact (`contact.html`)**: 24/7 Hotline & event inquiry form, service area map, emergency dispatch, operating hours, FAQ shortcuts, client reviews, CTA.

## AUTHENTICATION & DASHBOARD DESIGN
- **Auth (`login.html` & `register.html`)**: Clean centered card authentication with password visibility toggle and remember-me option.
- **Dashboard (`dashboard.html`)**: Customer Dashboard inspired by reference layout:
  - Topbar with user profile greeting, theme switcher, LTR/RTL switcher, and logout.
  - 8 Sidebar tabs: Overview, Book a Ride, My Reservations, Route & Stops, Booking History, Rewards, Payments, Profile.
  - Live 5-step status tracker (Requested -> Confirmed -> Chauffeur Assigned -> En Route -> Completed) with assigned driver card (Marcus Vance).
