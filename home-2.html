FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers before you reach out to our support team.

What is the minimum rental hour requirement?

Most weekend bookings require a 3 to 4 hour minimum rental period. Prom night packages have fixed 5-hour slots, while custom wedding convoys can be scheduled for split-time charter service.

Are drinks and champagne allowed on the party bus?

Yes! For guests 21+, BYOB is permitted on party lounge buses and stretch limousines. Ice, crystal glassware, and bottle coolers are provided complimentary in all vehicles.

How does the prom safety guarantee work?

All prom rentals enforce a strict zero-alcohol policy for minors, direct parent contact dispatching, and scheduled drop-off itineraries for complete parent peace of mind.

Is my service area covered for multi-home pickups?

Yes! We cover the entire metropolitan and surrounding suburban areas. Custom pickup stops at multiple guest locations can be specified directly inside your reservation.
