FAQ

Pricing Frequently Asked Questions

Transparent answers regarding flat rates, deposits, and payment options.

Are driver gratuity and taxes included in the quoted price?

Yes! All Velvet quotes include standard driver gratuity, fuel surcharges, and local taxes upfront. You will never encounter unexpected fees on your final bill.

How much deposit is required to lock in a reservation date?

A low 20% deposit is required upon reservation confirmation. The remaining balance can be paid in installments or settled 48 hours prior to departure.

Can we extend our rental time during the event?

Yes! Subject to chauffeur schedule availability, extra overtime hours can be authorized directly with your chauffeur or dispatch team at your standard hourly contract rate.

Can group members split payments for a party bus?

Yes! Our customer booking dashboard allows group leaders to send payment split links to multiple guests or parents for seamless collective payment.
